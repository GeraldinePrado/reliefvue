## Working versus planned

Working: local routes, sample contributions and receipts, synthetic eligibility/review state, province search/map, role-specific queues, charts, pagination, accounting reconciliation and tests.

Simulated: 10,440 households, 240 incident inputs, 192 AI checks-passed cases, 9,600 household payouts, reserve and approval evidence. These numbers are authored fixtures, not beneficiaries or AI accuracy.

Planned/unverified: live feeds, real AI, protected evidence storage, authentication and role authorization, persistent audit, production KYC/custody, wallet recovery, fiat integration, deployed audited reserve programme and on-chain duplicate-claim enforcement. Optional Devnet tools must be verified separately before any live transaction claim.
