# ReliefVue current scope: ideathon and hackathon submission

Updated from Geraldine's clarification, 2026-10-04. This scope governs the current task order. Technical design documents describe proposed architecture, not a requirement to finish a public relief service before pitching.

## What ReliefVue is now

An early-stage Thailand-first idea and explanatory prototype for emergency household grants on Solana, seeking possible funding through the ideathon/hackathon. Geraldine and Codex are the current working team. No users, donated reserve, investors, hired reviewers or senior developer exist yet. A possible award is conditional, not secured.

## The idea we are pitching

Prepare a SOL relief reserve before disasters. Use documented observed disaster evidence and human event approval to authorize a bounded response in a specific affected area. Give one fixed grant per privately verified household, with public transfer accounting and private identities. Separate relief funds from optional operating support.

The future grant benchmark uses Thai prices for three days of ready-to-eat essentials for a reference household of 3-5; it is not the pot divided by applicants. The exact baht amount is unvalidated. SOL retention or future baht conversion is the recipient's choice; conversion and recoverable wallets are future capabilities.

**Current fictional demo fixture (5 October 2026):** Geraldine selected 0.8 SOL per household for the Mae Rim walkthrough. The replay shows 9,600 sample households, 7,680 SOL in sample grants, a 12,000 SOL response budget, and 4,320 SOL still allocated. These figures are internally consistent examples, not a validated Thai essentials basket, government benefit equivalent, real reserve, or transfer history. A funded pilot would set a baht benchmark from dated local prices and determine the SOL amount near each payout.

## Current submission priorities, in order

1. **Pitch narrative:** problem, affected household, proposed solution, why Solana, defensible differentiation, and what funding would validate/build. Avoid claims of adoption, proven speed, audited custody or unique technology without evidence.
2. **One demo story:** a fictional Thailand flood moves from Watching to observed-impact review, simulated human authorization, one household grant and a public receipt view. Clearly label each simulation. Show a real Devnet receipt only after obtaining it; never imply a preview transferred funds.
3. **Prototype presentation:** make the screens communicate that story and show donors/households why the idea matters. Do not broaden into insurance, logistics, a general disaster oracle or a production admin system.
4. **Submission package:** concise pitch material, short demo script, screenshots/video and a readable public repository. Check the actual event's submission requirements before deciding which blockchain proof is mandatory.
5. **Conditional roadmap:** explain what funding buys, what remains uncertain and how an initial pilot could be evaluated.

## Technical proof is bounded

A small Devnet transaction flow may strengthen the hackathon demonstration. Compiling/deploying the proposed reserve contract is an optional next technical proof until the specific event's requirements establish otherwise. It must not block pitch preparation or turn into a production launch programme. No competition requirement has been verified here.

## Existing work we keep

The TypeScript/Vite/Solana Kit migration, local synthetic policy tests and labelled public preview are prototype groundwork. Anchor reserve source is exploratory and uncompiled/undeployed. Local staff roles are simulated; server rules do not lock a key holder out of the reserve. Existing groundwork does not establish production readiness and does not require us to complete every architecture feature now.

## Deferred until funding/pilot preparation

Real KYC and household investigations, staff hiring/coverage, production database/authentication, legal/custody/provider arrangements, automatic baht conversion, wallet recovery, live government/news ingestion and AI fact-checking, audited reserve governance, security assessment and real-money launch. Pricing/source research may support the pitch, but it does not establish a working payout policy.
