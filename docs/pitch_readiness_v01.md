# ReliefVue pitch readiness — time-boxed submission plan

## Assessment

The prototype is sufficient to begin the pitch deck now. Further dashboard expansion should not block storytelling. The current app can demonstrate the public mission, optional donor support, historical versus fictional incident records, a simulated AI-to-human evidence workflow, fixed household grants, supervisor exceptions and public accounting.

It is an explanatory prototype, not a production relief service. Synthetic volume demonstrates the intended workflow, not actual adoption, verified AI performance or operational readiness.

Start the deck in the next work block. Do not spend the remaining window building live AI, staff authentication or a new payout backend.

## Narrative outline

1. Hook: When disaster strikes, help should already be ready.
2. Household problem: different urgent needs, delayed assistance, evidence and accountability challenges. Use sourced evidence if quantified; do not invent national loss or unmet-demand statistics.
3. ReliefVue model: prepare a reserve, verify a response and household, deliver one fixed cash grant, trace funds.
4. Donor and household journey: demonstrate the actual public UI in two short paths.
5. Responsible verification: planned AI organizes provenance/time/location and flags replay or conflict; people authorize publication, events and eligibility under separate responsibilities.
6. Operations demo: show the 1,200-household fictional pipeline, 240 input fixtures, routine reviewer decisions and supervisor exceptions. Label all figures as simulation, not traction.
7. Why Solana: public transfer accounting and programmable grant constraints; neither blockchain nor AI proves real-world need. Reserve contract source is not a deployed audited program.
8. Execution plan and ask: what the award would fund, pilot scope, evidence policy, staff roles, provider/custody investigation and success measures. Exact amount and milestones need Geraldine's confirmation.
9. Team and closing: use confirmed team information; no invented hires, partnerships, approvals or adoption.

## Demo sequence

Homepage mission → donor optional support → historical/source-labelled map → reviewer AI attention (checks-passed versus human-review lane) → household evidence and locked grant → supervisor exception → public fund record. Keep the interactive event-authorization exercise separate from the populated operations snapshot.

## Claims to preserve

- Real: interface, routing, local state transitions, sample accounting, fixture search/pagination and tests.
- Simulated: household volume, incident feeds, AI outcomes, staff roles, identity evidence, audit history and operational payments.
- Planned: live government/news integration, AI evaluation, camera provenance and replay detection, protected document storage, authentication/authorization, persistent audit, production custody and baht conversion.
- Historical sources: evidence of the 2024 event only; not proof of ReliefVue operations or the fictional dashboard cases.
