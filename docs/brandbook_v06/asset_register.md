# ReliefVue brand guide v06 — assets and sources

- The elephant raster is the selected app identity asset. It is reproduced unchanged from `src/assets/brand/reliefvue-elephant-selected.png`. It is a brand symbol, not evidence of an official endorsement.
- The light wavescape is a decorative generated image based on Geraldine's supplied pastel reference. It contains no people, locations, country shape or transaction data.
- The separate Thailand outline uses Natural Earth 1:50m country geometry (public domain). It gives national context only. The current website's simulated incident map uses separate approximate area centres.
- The working palette follows the later selected identity and current application: ivory `#FDFBF7`, navy `#111D2D`, purple `#6840B5`, lavender `#A78BFA`, coral `#FF8B86`. The brighter Windows v02 palette, including mint, was a review candidate.
- Local font files are Alegreya Sans, Fraunces, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Thai Looped. Their OFL license files are retained alongside the assets.
- Product wording follows `src/main.ts`, `docs/design-brief.md` and `docs/brief.md`. The website's grant outcomes are fictional. The custom on-chain reserve has not been deployed; verified Solana signatures are a requirement of the proposed service.

Source for country outline: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries-2/
