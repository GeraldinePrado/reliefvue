# ReliefVue brand platform — v01

Recorded 1 November 2026. This is the canonical wording for the mission, vision, values and
tagline. The next brandbook and PRD revision should absorb this section; the repository's
current PRD is v0.4, so this is not a rollback to an earlier version.

## Tagline

**Relief that reaches. Trust you can trace.**

Use it as the public-facing line. It is the line in the film, the website header and the
brandbook opening.

## Vision

A world where humanitarian aid is free from corruption and misuse, where every contribution is
accounted for, and where help reaches the people who need it most — fairly, transparently and
with dignity.

## Mission

To protect humanitarian aid from corruption, fraud and misuse by building a transparent,
blockchain-powered relief network that connects donors, verified households and humanitarian
organizations.

Through secure identity verification, accountable fund management and human oversight,
ReliefVue works to ensure that emergency resources are distributed fairly, efficiently and for
their intended purpose.

## Core values

1. **Transparency.** Every donation, allocation and confirmed financial distribution should be
   traceable and accountable, without exposing beneficiaries' private information.
2. **Integrity.** Prevent corruption, diversion, duplicate claims, unauthorized spending and
   abuse of power through enforceable safeguards and independent oversight.
3. **Fairness.** Assistance is based on verified need and eligibility — not nationality,
   personal connections, favouritism or influence.
4. **Human dignity.** Protect people facing emergencies. Technology should make assistance
   easier to access, not punish legitimate recipients or remove their right to appeal.
5. **Accountability without borders.** Build a globally accessible system where humanitarian
   organizations, donors and relief operators are held to clear, verifiable standards.

## Anti-corruption at three levels

| Level | What ReliefVue does |
| --- | --- |
| **Before distribution** | Verify organizers, households and eligibility. Check official disaster declarations and observed impact. Define the response area and budget through independent human review. |
| **During distribution** | Prevent unauthorized transfers, double allocations, fund diversion and prohibited purchases through supported payment routes, 2-of-3 approvals and one household = one wallet. |
| **After distribution** | Reconcile financial records, investigate discrepancies, support appeals and make verified results auditable on Solana. |

Blockchain provides verifiable financial records. It cannot independently prove that every
real-world claim or purchase was legitimate. That is why ReliefVue also uses KYC, accountable
human decisions, supported payment controls and independent oversight.

## The principle that matters most

> ReliefVue is not built on the assumption that disaster victims are dishonest. It is built on
> the belief that humanitarian assistance deserves protection from corruption and abuse at
> every level, including the people and institutions responsible for managing it.

This distinction is the credibility test for beneficiaries, donors, NGOs and government
partners. Keep it visible in the brandbook, the PRD and the public explanation.
