**Submission requirement supplied by Geraldine:** a separate product demo video on YouTube, Loom or Vimeo, up to 3 minutes. Show the live product. Do not use a slide deck or code walkthrough.

## Recording path (target 2:30–2:45)

| Time | Show on the live website | Say in one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Homepage and visible **DEMO MODE** label | “ReliefVue prepares household relief before floods and makes fund movements easy to follow.” |
| 0:15–0:40 | Donor route and reserve explanation | “A donor can see what the proposed reserve is for and how it would be used.” |

| 2:25–2:45 | Fund activity and prototype boundary | “These records are fictional. The product goal is a public Solana receipt for each confirmed transfer without exposing household details.” |

## Recording checks

- Record the deployed site in a browser, with the pointer and page content visible. Keep the **DEMO MODE** label in the opening shot.
- Use made-up household information. Do not enter personal documents, seed phrases or real identity numbers.
- Keep fictional grant and duplicate screens described as a walkthrough. Only say a Devnet transfer happened if the recording shows its actual confirmed signature and Explorer link.
- Keep the public pitch video separate: that video introduces Geraldine and why she is building ReliefVue.
- Before recording, verify the live Vercel revision, map, donor route, household route and reviewer route at the exact URL used in the video.
