01 / FOUNDATION

Credibility must be earned in the interface.

The previous prototype’s pale green cards and anonymous diamond felt soft and generic. ReliefVue needs a distinctive but quiet identity, coupled with exact language about what is verified, simulated, pending, and still planned.

01 / DONOR

See the whole fund path

Reserve, event allocation, confirmed payout, and remaining balance must reconcile. A transaction hash is evidence only after the network confirms it.

02 / HOUSEHOLD

Understand the decision

Eligibility, denial, review, and payout states explain why and what comes next without exposing personal details in public records.

03 / OPERATOR

Approve with a source

A supplied notice and AI suggestion remain advisory. A human checks the source, area, date, and uncertainty before opening claims.

Brand thesis: prepared relief with visible accountability. The visual system should feel as dependable as a financial record and as legible as emergency guidance. It should never imply that the prototype already has a production verification or payment partner.

02 / COLOR

Five colors. Different jobs.

Your hex values form the digital direction. They are not Pantone specifications; print matching needs a later physical proof. The brighter colors are accents and states, while navy and off-white carry most of the reading experience.

Deep navy#0E1626Foundation, dark surfaces, primary ink
Solana purple#9945FFLedger lines, verification accent
Rescue coral#FF5A5FActive relief CTA and critical alert
Confirmed mint#14F195Completed allocation and success
Off-white#F9FAFBReading canvas and tables

Use the bright colors with restraint

Coral belongs on the primary Donate action during an active response and on critical alerts. Use navy for ordinary actions. Purple identifies ledger and verification information. Mint indicates confirmed completion only, never eligibility that is merely predicted.

Suggested text variants

For readable small text on off-white, use purple #7130BD and mint green #007A4D. For ledger text on navy, use #B985FF. Keep the supplied hex values for accents and larger visual signals.

I recommend #14F195 over #00F5FF for the success state. It reads more clearly as completion and stays distinct from purple ledger evidence. This choice is a recommendation, not a replacement of your stated option until you confirm it.

Give the name a reason to be remembered.

The prototype’s Unicode diamond has no clear connection to readiness, direct aid, or accountability. 05 / VOICE

Reassuring without making promises it cannot keep.

ACTION

“Give help” / “Get help”

Use a human first choice. At the payment step, name the actual amount, currency, destination, and transaction network.

ELIGIBILITY

“Your recorded home is inside the response area.”

Explain the reason and the source of the check. Do not call a synthetic profile identity verified.

EVIDENCE

“Donation confirmed on Solana Devnet.”

Say this only after confirmation, with the real signature and a working Explorer link. Preview and pending states use their own words.

06 / IMAGERY

Put real life beside the ledger.

Real, consented community photographs are the primary image language. Show active relief, rebuilding, and delivery with warmth and dignity. Never use AI-generated humans or disaster scenes. No approved photographs are in this workspace, so the frames here deliberately contain no synthetic stand-ins.

Approved real photograph requiredHero placement · community action or rebuilding · consent, source and context recorded

PHOTOGRAPHY STANDARD

Hopeful, never staged

Choose people taking action, receiving useful support, or rebuilding together. Use a subtle warm treatment against navy, preserving natural skin tones and truthful context. Avoid disaster spectacle, heavy filters, stock-style posing, and any crop that changes the meaning of the event.

Record photographer, location/date, consent, permitted use, and caption. If those facts are unavailable, hold the image rather than implying it documents ReliefVue delivery.

Ledger to life / proof pipeline

Use this four-stage view only when each stage has real evidence. The NGO-wallet and supplies path is a future partner scenario, not the current direct-household Devnet prototype.

07 / EXPERIENCE ARCHITECTURE

Let people find the right path immediately.

The public first viewport shows the active response or reserve state and two equal-entry journeys. The operator route remains separate. Both public journeys need complete empty, blocked, pending, confirmed, and recovery states.

01 / OVERVIEWChoose a task

Reserve status, event state, Give help, Get help, and Fund activity.

02 / DONATEKnow the destination

Relief amount → optional platform support → separate totals → confirmation → receipt.

03 / REQUESTSee the reason

Profile → area → eligibility → available payout → receipt or recovery.

Notice source → AI suggestion → uncertainty → human decision.

A current GPS reading cannot substitute for a verified home area or exclude an evacuated household. Public transaction trails must hide names, addresses, and documents. The app cannot claim on-chain duplicate enforcement until a deployed program proves it.

09 / UI SYSTEM

Design rules that survive implementation.

Confirmed transferActual transaction verified; show amount, time, signature, and Explorer link.
Pending confirmationKeep the action from being repeated. State what is being checked and when to retry.
Already receivedExplain the household/event rule and the available review path.

Layout + accessibility

Task first, one clear main action per step, stable button sizes, visible keyboard focus, persistent labels, and text for every status. Reflow at 320 px and 200% zoom. Do not rely on color to explain outcomes.

Data and copy

Keep monetary units explicit, use tabular figures in dense metrics, wrap long hashes without losing the full value, and label sample data. Reserve, allocated, distributed, and remaining amounts must reconcile.
