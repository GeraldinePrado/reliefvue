## Pitch premise

**When disaster strikes, help should already be ready.**

ReliefVue prepares a relief reserve, privately reviews affected households, and aims to deliver a fixed grant with publicly traceable transfers on Solana. Households have different urgent needs; cash can let them choose essentials where purchasing is safe and available. The current public product is a labelled simulation, not an operating relief service.

## Confirmed responsibilities

Fact checkers investigate incident authenticity, time, place, corroboration and conflicts. Household reviewers approve routine eligible applications; supervisors handle disputed duplication, fraud flags and exceptions. Executive policy sets the disaster budget and fixed amount; staff cannot edit it. Disaster-event authorization remains a separate decision. A reviewer action or AI flag alone does not move money.

Identity/residence/household evidence is private. Planned camera-only evidence should flag possible photographed photos or screens for human review; it cannot guarantee authenticity. No real camera or AI model is operating in this mockup.

## Eligibility and expansion

Thailand-first; people physically in Thailand with a qualifying disaster connection may receive, including lawful foreign residents under a future reviewed policy. Usual residence and temporary evacuation location remain distinct. Domestic displacement or precautionary evacuation need human review; unemployment or an unrelated move alone does not qualify. People outside Thailand may donate, not receive under the Thailand programme.

Existing wallets, assisted wallet setup and baht-provider access are intended routes. Country-by-country expansion, organization dashboards/APIs, optional MCP access, multisig executive controls and institutional licensing belong in roadmap/Q&A. They are not built integrations or signed partnerships.

## Evidence and technology boundaries

Planned AI should organize original official notices, weather observations, local authority/governor channels, news reports and manual submissions. Check provenance, time, location, independence, duplicate/replay cues and conflicting evidence. A weather forecast is not proof of household loss. Historical source links document the event only; they never prove ReliefVue operations.

The public demonstration is local state. Optional Devnet tools are separate. The reserve Anchor source is uncompiled, undeployed and unaudited according to chain/README.md; no on-chain enforcement claim is supported by the current web walkthrough. Solana transfers do not establish real-world identity or need.

## Solana in the main story

Opening product sentence: “ReliefVue is a disaster-ready relief platform being built on Solana, preparing funds before disaster and connecting verified households with direct, traceable grants.” The current public app is a simulation; phrase programme enforcement and production settlement as intended architecture.

Main pitch: preparedness problem; reserve-to-household model; why Solana; private verification and human review; working demonstration; focused roadmap and ask. Supporting sections: beginner wallet/baht options, lawful-resident and displacement policy, institutional API/MCP, shared treasury governance, duplicate prevention and assisted access. Avoid crowding the three-minute narrative with every future integration.
