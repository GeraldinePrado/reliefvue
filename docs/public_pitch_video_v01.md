# ReliefVue — public founder pitch video

**Target:** about 1 minute 45 seconds at a steady pace (190 words). **Limit:** 2 minutes. Speak to camera. This is Geraldine's introduction and reason for building ReliefVue; the separate product demo will show the live website.

## Spoken script

Hi, I’m Geraldine, founder of The Glow Up Code. I help women use AI without feeling overwhelmed, so they have more choices beyond a nine-to-five job. I grew up in the Philippines without a computer or internet. Access is personal to me.

I saw how families far from town could miss relief when they could not reach a distribution point. Help did not always match their needs.

That is why I’m building ReliefVue: emergency funds ready before disaster. I’m starting in Thailand, where I now live. Donors would fund a reserve early. Households would be reviewed privately, then approved families would receive one grant for what they actually need. Solana would make confirmed transfers traceable while identities stay private. Human review would limit duplicate claims and misuse. The same system of reserve rules and public records could later help NGOs, foundations, philanthropists and public institutions run accountable relief programs.

I built the donor, household, reviewer and public fund prototype alone, using fictional cases. The next proof is a Devnet grant and duplicate rejection.

We cannot stop every disaster. We can prepare sooner, reach more households, and show where help went.
