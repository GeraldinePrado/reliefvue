# ReliefVue — presentation prototype brief

## Ownership and status

- Ownership: shared client/colleague project, confirmed by Geraldine on 2026-10-02.
- Status: proposed prototype direction; no production implementation or payout partner is approved.
- Source: the numbered ReadyFund handover documents in this project, adjusted by Geraldine's conversation decisions on 2026-10-02.

## Product idea

Keep a disaster-relief reserve funded before an emergency. After an authorized operator verifies an official affected-area notice, eligible households in that area can request direct help. Donors can see reserve, allocation, and payout movements without seeing household identities.

## Three-hour presentation target

Show one scripted scenario: reserve funded before a mock Bangkok flood declaration; a donor adds test funds; a Bangkok demo household is approved and receives a simulated payout; the same household's second claim is rejected; a Chiang Mai household cannot claim the Bangkok allocation. Show a transparent activity trail.

## Honest boundaries

- All balances, declarations, household checks, and payouts in the prototype are simulations.
- No real identity documents, government connection, fiat conversion, or Solana transaction are included.
- A real product would need an authorized household verifier and a qualified payout partner for baht delivery.
- An on-chain repeat-claim rule would only prevent reuse of the same verified household identifier; it would not verify a real household.

## Presentation requirements

- Name: ReliefVue (working name).
- Two obvious actions: Give help and Get help.
- Clear DEMO labels and human-readable blocked/rejected states.
- Responsive layout and keyboard-usable controls.
- Local-only prototype; external publication and Drive sync require a separate request.
