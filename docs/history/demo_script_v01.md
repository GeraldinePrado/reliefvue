# ReadyFund — 90-Second Demo Script

## Opening

> "Disaster fundraising usually starts after the disaster has already happened. ReadyFund changes that: we keep emergency money ready before disaster strikes."

## Show reserve

Open dashboard.

> "This reserve is already funded and transparent."

Point to:
- reserve balance
- active disaster status

## Activate scenario

> "For this demo, Chiang Mai Flood 2026 has been officially activated."

Clearly show **DEMO** labeling.

## Donor action

Connect donor wallet.

Donate a small amount of Devnet SOL.

> "Anyone can add to the reserve, and the contribution is a real Solana Devnet transaction."

Show confirmed signature.

## Household verification

Switch to recipient flow.

> "Identity and residency would be verified privately off-chain. We never put passports or home addresses on Solana."

Show mock checks:

- identity verified
- residency verified
- affected zone verified
- no prior claim

## Claim

Press **Claim relief**.

> "This household now receives its allocation directly."

Show:
- success
- recipient wallet
- transaction signature

## Fraud / duplicate moment

Attempt claim again with the same household ID.

Show:

> **Claim rejected — this household has already received assistance for this disaster.**

Say:

> "The household claim receipt is deterministic on Solana, so creating a new account or wallet does not create another entitlement."

## Close

> "ReadyFund is not another post-disaster crowdfunding site. It is pre-funded emergency infrastructure: money is ready before the emergency, private eligibility happens off-chain, and distribution is auditable on Solana."

Final line:

> **Emergency money ready before disaster.**
