# ReadyFund — Open Questions After the MVP

Do not solve these before the core demo works.

## Product / governance

1. Who legally owns or controls the long-lived emergency reserve?
2. Which government sources can activate a disaster?
3. What geographic precision is required?
4. Who decides the per-household allocation?
5. What happens when funds are insufficient?
6. What happens when the reserve has excess funds?
7. How long is a claim window?
8. What is the appeal process for rejected households?
9. Can one address contain multiple legitimate households?
10. How are displaced people verified when documents are lost?

## Fraud / identity

11. What KYC provider can legally operate for the target market?
12. How do we construct a privacy-preserving unique household nullifier?
13. What data is retained and for how long?
14. Which fraud signals can trigger review without unfair automatic denial?
15. How do we recover if a verified recipient loses a wallet?
16. How do we avoid multiple wallets / identities funneling to one fraud ring?

## Money / regulation

17. Does the reserve hold fiat, stablecoins, or both?
18. Who is the regulated custodian?
19. How do recipients receive usable THB?
20. Which licenses / fundraising permissions are required?
21. Can foreign donors participate?
22. Are donations tax-deductible, and under what partner structure?
23. What AML / Travel Rule obligations apply?
24. How are refunds / chargebacks handled?

## Business model

25. Optional donor tips?
26. SaaS fee to NGOs/foundations?
27. Enterprise CSR / white-label emergency reserve?
28. API / audit reporting fees?
29. Fraud / verification infrastructure fees?
30. Corporate reserve matching tools?

## Scale

31. Thailand only first?
32. Province-specific reserve vs national reserve?
33. Multi-disaster concurrent claims?
34. Can the protocol support non-cash aid later?
35. Can disaster-need signals guide allocations without exposing recipients?

## Differentiation

Continuously test the product against:
- disaster crowdfunding platforms
- NGO cash-assistance systems
- blockchain humanitarian-aid projects
- government direct-payment systems

The differentiation should remain:

> **Pre-funded emergency liquidity + private household verification + one-claim enforcement + transparent settlement.**
